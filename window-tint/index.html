Automotive tint FAQs

What is the difference between carbon and ceramic tint?

Both can provide privacy, glare reduction, and a finished appearance. Ceramic film is the premium option when heat rejection and cabin comfort are higher priorities.

Can I tint only my front two windows?

Yes. Front-window matching is available for customers who want the front glass to better coordinate with factory-tinted rear glass.

Can Art remove my existing tint?

Yes. Existing film can be removed, including old film that has started bubbling, peeling, discoloring, or otherwise failing.

How do I choose a shade?

Shade choice depends on the look, privacy, visibility, and coverage you want. Art can help you choose an option while you remain responsible for complying with applicable tint laws.
