PPF FAQs

What areas can be covered with PPF?

Common areas include the front bumper, hood, fenders, mirrors, headlights, door cups, A-pillars, and other high-impact or custom areas.

Why isn't PPF priced online?

Pricing depends heavily on the vehicle, panel shape, film coverage, and requested installation. Art quotes PPF individually so the price matches the actual job.

Does PPF change the appearance of the paint?

PPF is intended to provide clear protection while preserving the underlying finish. Exact appearance can vary with the film and the condition of the paint.
